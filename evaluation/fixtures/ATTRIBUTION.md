Original files are preserved byte-for-byte; SHA256 and source metadata accompany
them. Evaluation derivatives are temporal crops resampled to 8fps, resized,
encoded, and in two cases transposed with display-rotation metadata. Raw model
and composite derivatives replace the selected object or character, as described
in each case's provenance and graph. Retain the corresponding source license,
credit, and change notice when sharing those derivatives. Procedural cube/ball
and character drawings are original MIT-licensed project fixtures.
